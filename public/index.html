YOUR AI GAME COMPANION

The AI companion lives inside the Riddle Game. It can offer hints, explain a riddle after you finish, suggest similar challenges, and help you practice — without playing the puzzle for you.

AI assistance is currently available in the Riddle Game experience, not as a chatbot on this page and not across every title yet.
