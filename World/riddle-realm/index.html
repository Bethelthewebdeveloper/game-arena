FAQ

Is the free game complete?

Yes. Core modes, daily challenge, progression, and basic AI stay available on Free.

What does Pro add?

Higher AI limits, advanced AI tools, practice helpers, and Pro cosmetics. No gameplay power paywall.

Can I pay now?

Not yet. Checkout is prepared for a future payment provider. No cards are collected in this version.

What is Demo Pro Mode?

A Settings switch for developers to test Pro features. It is not a purchase and must stay off in production.

How do I restore Pro later?

Restore will use your account + payment provider verification once accounts and billing exist.
